About

Engineering dependable robotic intelligence.

I focus on building software systems that bring robotic platforms from research concepts to operational reality. My work includes embedded software, mobile autonomy, sensor integration, motion control, and performance validation in constrained environments.

My goal is to turn real-world complexity into reliable, testable, scalable software. I enjoy solving problems across the full stack—from low-level drivers and perception pipelines to navigation, planning, and user-facing interfaces.
